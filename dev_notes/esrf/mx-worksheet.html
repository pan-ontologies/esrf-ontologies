Step 1 · five minutes

One pattern, no hierarchy drawing

Every technique in ESRFET is a single EquivalentClasses axiom: experimental_technique intersected with a list of ∃ property . value restrictions. Nothing is ever asserted as a subclass. The reasoner works out that SCXRD ⊑ XRD purely because SCXRD's list contains XRD's list plus one more conjunct.

// already in the ontology — this is the template for everything below SCXRD ≡ experimental_technique ⊓ ∃ requires_sample_input . x-ray ⊓ ∃ measures_result_of_interaction . diffraction_of_elastic_scattering ⊓ ∃ requires_sample_type . single_crystal_sample ← the only delta vs. XRD

Step 3 · the one definition that matters

Define MX once

MX is currently an empty stub — declared a subclass of experimental_technique and nothing more. Get these lines agreed and the rest of the family costs one line each.

// PROPOSAL — confirm each line with the scientist, then delete or extend MX ≡ experimental_technique ⊓ ∃ requires_sample_input . x-ray ← inherited from XRD, uncontroversial ⊓ ∃ measures_result_of_interaction . diffraction_of_elastic_scattering ← inherited from XRD ⊓ ∃ requires_sample_type . single_crystal_sample ← makes MX ⊑ SCXRD. agreed? ⊓ ∃ requires_sample_type . biological_sample ← the actual MX delta ⊓ ∃ measures_with_a_detector_type . area_detector ← definitional, or just true today? ⊓ ∃ requires_sample_input_property . cryogenic_temperature ← definitional, or just typical? room-temp MX exists ⊓ ∃ measures_interaction_result_property . small_length_scales ← 1–10 Å, i.e. atomic. needed?

The two lines to argue about are the last two. A restriction in an EquivalentClasses axiom is definitional: anything without it is not MX. If room-temperature MX counts as MX, cryogenic_temperature cannot go here — it belongs on the child techniques instead. Ask the question in exactly those terms and the answer comes fast.

Step 5 · park these, do not solve them live

Eight things the vocabulary cannot say yet

Each of these blocks at least one MX technique. Read them out, capture the scientist's answer, and resolve them after the meeting — they need new classes, not new axioms.

Gap 1 · blocks SAD

No class for a single incident energy

space_time offers detection_as_a_function_of_limited_number_of_incident_energies, but nothing distinguishes one energy from several. SAD and MAD would get identical definitions and the reasoner would declare them equivalent.

Gap 2 · blocks TR-MX, TR-SSX

No reaction trigger

laser exists as a sample_input, so pump-probe is expressible. Mix-and-inject, temperature jump and electric-field triggers have no vocabulary at all.

Gap 3 · blocks SMX

Beam size is not sample spatial resolution

micro_sample_spatial_resolution means the sample is resolved at that scale — it appears in scanning techniques. MX with a micro-focus beam does not resolve the crystal spatially; it just uses a small beam. There is no term for beam size.

Gap 4 · blocks SMX, TR-SSX

No sample delivery vocabulary

Fixed target, chip, viscous jet, injector, loop. On the beamlines these are the real distinctions between serial methods, and none of them are representable.

Gap 5 · blocks MX

Two sample types at once

MX needs biological_sample and single_crystal_sample. The property allows it, but no existing technique does this — confirm the intersection is intended rather than a modelling error.

Gap 6 · cosmetic

Two typos in IRIs

simultanious_reference and sample_spatial_resolution_achived_on_input. Renaming an IRI is breaking, so decide now whether to fix before v1.0 or keep and add a correct skos:prefLabel.

Gap 7 · consistency

One property has the wrong domain

measures_transmission_with_detector_type has domain transmission; the other seven have domain experimental_technique. Either it is a deliberate exception or it should be folded into measures_with_a_detector_type.

Gap 8 · scope

Is phasing a technique?

ESRFET models what is measured. MAD and SAD are named for how the data is used to solve the phase problem. The measurement of SAD is just MX at a chosen energy. Worth asking whether they belong in this ontology at all.
